# Owner analytics

The owner dashboard is a separate responsive experience at `/admin/`, not a fifth archive tab. It uses the same warm paper, ink, terracotta, border, typography, and focus tokens as What I Made, with moderately denser information layout.

- Default to 30 days and keep 7-day, 30-day, 90-day, and 12-month options visible.
- Lead with six plain-language totals, then a labeled activity trend, its equivalent data table, and the invited-account list.
- User rows are large buttons that open a native modal detail view containing metadata-only activity.
- Charts supplement text and tables. Exact values never depend on color, hover, or pointer precision.
- Use “Cooks recorded” and “Ideas saved,” not “posts,” engagement scores, streaks, leaderboards, or comparative rankings.
- Every loading, empty, offline, partial-data, denied, clearing, and failure state explains what the owner can do next.
- Destructive analytics clearing requires typing `ERASE ANALYTICS` and must state that accounts and local archives are unaffected.
- Maintain 44×44-pixel controls, visible focus, enlarged-text reflow, dark mode, reduced motion, safe areas, and no page-level horizontal overflow.
