# Public sample archive

## Purpose

Help an invited visitor understand the emotional and functional value of What I Made before committing to sign-in. Use the established culinary field-journal interface and real navigation rather than marketing illustrations.

## Composition

- Signed-out hierarchy: mark, invitation context, concise value statement, primary **Explore a sample archive**, secondary **Sign in with email**, invitation helper.
- Demo chrome: a compact persistent strip carries the app mark, **Sample archive**, **Fictional · Read only**, and a 44-pixel **Sign in** action.
- Year orientation: a photographic product-tour card summarizes the fictional year and provides direct, descriptive entry points to the culinary Map, searchable Journal, month-grouped photo recap, and Ideas. It explicitly states that sample content never enters the visitor's archive.
- Production shell: hide the desktop prototype scenario panel by default. It remains available only through the explicit `prototype=1` development parameter.
- Personal-action modal: native modal semantics, invitation explanation, **Sign in with invited email**, and **Keep exploring**. Restore focus to the originating control.

## Behavior and accessibility

All existing tab, map, filter, recap, and detail interactions remain available. Mutating actions do not disappear, because they help explain capability; they open the invitation dialog instead. Keep the Backup card hidden. Labels must say “fictional” or “sample” where context could otherwise be mistaken for personal history. Maintain safe areas, visible focus, 44×44-pixel targets, reduced motion, enlarged text, and no page-level overflow.
